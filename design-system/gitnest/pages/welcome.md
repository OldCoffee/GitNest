# Welcome page overrides

> Brand-first entry surface; still shares GitNest tokens.

## Rules
- Hero may use `--jb-radius-xl` and stronger brand typography
- Accent remains GitNest Blue (`#3D7EFF`) — not green
- Graphite neutrals for cards/panels
- CTAs use `Button variant="primary"`
- Confirmations use app `ConfirmDialog` via `uiConfirm` (not Tauri/native confirm)
- Keep one dominant composition in the first viewport
- Avoid purple gradients, glow stacks, and emoji icons
- Logo branch nodes stay in the blue family (no success-green accents)
